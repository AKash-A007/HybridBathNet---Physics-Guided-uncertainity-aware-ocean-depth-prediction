User authorized the proposed experiment to reduce interval width while retaining coverage. Keep GEBCO only and full HybridBathNet physics/depth/uncertainty. No Stumpf/RF/CNN baselines. Existing447-train/123-calibration/208-validation/343-test indices and original test geography remain fixed; primary test0–20m has228 centres. Historical checkpoints and root metrics are immutable.

Exclude training points with shared native GEBCO interpolation support or centre distance<96px from that fold's held region. Refit all engineered feature references and normalization on remaining fold training footprints; exclude calibration/validation/test footprints. No warm start from checkpoints trained on held labels.

Train one full model per fold from scratch, seeds42/43/44, same joint Huber1+Gaussian NLL1+extinction.03+reconstruction.1, spatial uncertainty head, separate variance clipping, max180/patience30, validation-selected EMA. The existing external validation may select checkpoints; OOF held labels never select them. These fold models generate error examples, not the final prediction ensemble. Smaller fold training sets may exaggerate risk; this is a documented limitation.
3. Train a compact spatial uncertainty correction head on OOF features and residuals. Use semantically aligned imagery/depth/Kd/z_ext/head-sigma rather than arbitrary encoder embeddings from independently initialized models. Predict a positive location-dependent multiplier of total variance, using Gaussian NLL with equal2km group weight. Train up to200 head epochs; choose checkpoint by validation NLL with patience30, including the zero-correction identity checkpoint at epoch0. Source inputs/variance are detached from gradients. The existing three full-data GEBCO members stay frozen, so their mean depth/RMSE is unchanged. OOF single-member residuals can differ from final ensemble errors; independent calibration will assess/adjust their scale.
4. Refit only a global sigma multiplier on the123 independent calibration locations; freeze before final test evaluation. Compare original globally calibrated total sigma against the learned-head version, reporting raw and calibrated variants, validation/test coverage, width, NLL, CRPS,90% interval score, error ranking, all depth bins and spatial-bootstrap paired intervals. No held-out removal, clipping or target-dependent test choice. A narrower interval at worse coverage does not establish improvement.

The correction head predicts total residual risk, including possible shared bias; it does not identify a new pure aleatoric variance. Preserve/export original uncertainty-head aleatoric and deep-ensemble epistemic components separately. Physics remains active in all depth models and final source ensemble; optical masks remain disabled. Default/deployment selection remains unchanged until evidence is reviewed. This is one predefined experiment, not test-driven hyperparameter search.
