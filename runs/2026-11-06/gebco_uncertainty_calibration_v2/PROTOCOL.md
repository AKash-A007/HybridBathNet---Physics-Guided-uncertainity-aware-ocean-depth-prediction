# GEBCO uncertainty calibration study — predefined protocol

User authorizes improving uncertainty using literature. Preserve all physics/depth/head/ensemble weights, features, test geography and predictions. Reuse independent seeds42/1/7 and the123 calibration locations in15 approximately2km groups excluded from network and feature fitting. No ATL24 or tabular model training.

Primary candidate: group-balanced Gaussian variance scaling fitted by calibration NLL; analytic multiplier squared = equally weighted group mean of squared standardized residuals. Secondary fixed candidates: equal-group empirical signed-residual calibration (CRUDE adaptation) and normalized hierarchical conformal prediction (HCP). Existing raw Gaussian intervals and maximum-per-group conformal intervals are controls. Methods are declared before new evaluation; no candidate parameters are fitted to validation/test errors.

For HCP, each calibration group has mass1/(G+1), distributed equally among its points, plus mass1/(G+1) at+infinity. This targets a point in a new exchangeable group, not simultaneous coverage of all pixels. With15 calibration groups, finite95% HCP intervals are unsupported. Spatial blocks do not guarantee hierarchical exchangeability; empirical coverage must be reported honestly. CRUDE and scaling receive no distribution-free coverage claim.

First fit and perform leave-one-calibration-group-out diagnostics. Freeze full-calibration states before loading validation/test predictions. Compare68/80/90% coverage and width,90% proper interval score, Gaussian/empirical CRPS where a distribution exists, depth bins, pixel- and equal-group averages, spatial-bootstrap differences and confidence intervals. Primary reporting domain is0–20m, fixed228 test locations; report0–25m sensitivity separately. No test filtering, selection of a target-dependent operating threshold, depth correction or change of reported RMSE. Calibration modifies total predictive intervals; raw aleatoric/epistemic components retain their original meaning.

References: [Levi et al., regression uncertainty calibration](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9330317/), [CRUDE](https://arxiv.org/html/2005.12496v2), [Lee et al., hierarchical data](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.06342v4), [shared Christofilakos et al. SDB uncertainty paper](https://elib.dlr.de/216314/1/remotesensing-17-03060.pdf). Calibration and proper interval assessment follow these ideas; spatial weighting is explicitly our adaptation where indicated. No guarantee of improvement or true-seabed accuracy is assumed.
