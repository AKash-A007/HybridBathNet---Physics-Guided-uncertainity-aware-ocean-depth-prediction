# Nearshore error and independent-depth audit, 6 November 2026

## Scope and decision

This is an exploratory audit of the frozen, validation-selected `nearshore_hybrid_v1` model. The original train/validation/test regions, GEBCO targets, predictions, checkpoint and reported RMSE are unchanged. The root `results/hybridbathnet_results.json` remains historical. No training was launched. Test-residual inspection makes subsequent changes targeted to these groups exploratory; select future models on validation and obtain a new independent geographic assessment before confirmatory claims.

## 10–15 m error and image quality

The saved test 10–15 m bin has 64 patch centres, RMSE **3.255 m** and mean prediction bias **−1.863 m**. Four approximate 2 km cells contribute **76.4%** of that bin's squared error. The corresponding validation bin has 64 centres and RMSE **1.665 m**. [Full machine-readable audit](quality_audit.json) gives all split/bin proxy summaries and group contributions.

The image-composite and original per-date Sentinel-2 scene-classification layers (SCL) were sampled at each patch centre, across **12 distinct acquisition dates**. Test 10–15 m centres have median **6 SCL-clear dates** and **2 cloud/shadow dates**, versus **8** and **0** for other 0–20 m test centres. Their median blue/green composite temporal-std norm is **0.0202** versus **0.0159**; median valid-patch GEBCO depth range is **2.78 m** versus **2.22 m**. These are associations, not diagnosed causes. Two of the four highest-error groups have **12 clear dates and zero cloud/shadow dates** at their centres; therefore centre cloud classification alone does not explain the errors. Glint, turbidity, bottom type and local reference accuracy remain unresolved. SCL is a centre-only classification; temporal std includes changing water conditions and is not sensor noise. The GEBCO patch range is an interpolation/terrain proxy, not a native reference-quality flag.

## Independent ATL24 reference discovery for the actual AOI

The AOI query used **76.30–76.55° E, 8.90–9.20° N**, January–April 2024. NASA CMR lists **four ATL24 V002 granule footprints** intersecting that bbox ([candidate manifest](atl24_cmr_candidates.json)); a track-footprint intersection does not guarantee bottom returns inside the study area. The first unauthenticated responses were HTML login pages, never accepted as `.h5` data. The user then saved a separate Earthdata token outside the repository; all [four official V002 granules](atl24_v002_download_status.json) were downloaded and HDF5-signature-verified. The temporary plaintext token file was removed after download. [Official AOI extraction](atl24_v002_extraction.json) contains 562,626 classified/unclassified photon rows inside the exact bbox, but only **52 bathymetry-class photons**, of which **32** meet confidence≥0.6 and 0–20 m depth. The [AOI photon parquet](atl24_v002_aoi_all_photons.parquet) preserves all classes and quality fields.

A public [SlideRule `atl24x` AOI subset](atl24_bathymetry_raw.parquet) returned **95 bathymetry-class photons** with confidence at least 0.6. All returned coordinates are within the requested bbox (lon **76.3250–76.5456**, lat **8.9289–9.1892**). They come from **three track/cycle/region combinations**, dated **7 February–8 April 2024**; **61** have finite depths in 0–20 m. The spatially audited points are [here](atl24_bathymetry_audited.parquet) and [summarized here](atl24_reference_audit.json). SlideRule's current catalogue lists V003 input granules, so this subset is **not** a verified NSIDC V002 extract. The service emitted a metadata-parse warning while still returning points; source version and quality flags should be checked against official granules before scientific use. [NSIDC ATL24 product](https://nsidc.org/data/atl24/versions/2) and [SlideRule field definitions](https://docs.slideruleearth.io/user-guide/icesat2) document the product and confidence, height, uncertainty and flag fields.

Only **12/61** shallow ATL24 photons lie where corrected GEBCO is also 0–25 m; none of the ATL24 10–15 m photons meet that GEBCO shelf condition. No shallow ATL24 photon is within **100 m of a current training centre**; only **three** lie within 100 m of current test centres. ATL24-minus-GEBCO at all 61 shallow ATL24 points has median absolute difference **13.27 m**, but many points are outside the GEBCO shallow shelf or over GEBCO land, and photons are clustered along tracks. This is a **reference-coverage and disagreement signal**, not a model RMSE or proof that either source is wrong. ATL24 instantaneous sea-surface-to-bottom depths, GEBCO's coarse grid and their geolocation/datum/epoch differences require careful comparison. The existing validation/test blocks and GEBCO labels must not be altered based on these inspected outcomes.

## Full-archive AOI subset and separate training

The official 2024 V002 bathymetry subset is too sparse for spatially distinct training and evaluation. A second public SlideRule query used the **same bbox** across October 2018–November 2024. It returned [3,189 AOI bathymetry-class points](atl24_archive_bathymetry_raw.parquet), 2,757 with 0–20 m depth. The service reported `invalid dataframe` for some source granules, so its subset may be incomplete; current SlideRule metadata advertises V003 inputs and is **not** a direct V002 extract. [Archive spatial audit](atl24_archive_reference_audit.json) and [split feasibility](atl24_training_feasibility.json) document coverage and fit to the fixed study blocks.

A distinct [ATL24-supervised HybridBathNet experiment](../../2026-11-06/atl24_hybrid_v1/REPORT.md) was completed on aggregated 10 m pixel labels from this larger archive. After declared quality, optical and spatial-buffer checks, the dataset has **570/112/233** train/validation/test centres. Validation RMSE was **3.443 m** and test RMSE **4.741 m** at 0–20 m; its report has per-bin errors, uncertainty and limitations. The original GEBCO model/data/test result remain unchanged. These reference-specific scores are not directly comparable.

## Next use of the reference

The four official V002 granules have been checked. The broader public V003 subset supported a first separate feasibility training run, but further work needs source completeness/version verification, ATL24 classification/confidence/horizontal and vertical uncertainty, sensor-depth flags, neighbouring sea-surface returns, spatial clustering, and Sentinel acquisition proximity. More photons along one track are not independent areas. Do not merge this dataset into the prior GEBCO benchmark or train against the already inspected test-bin outcomes.

**Checks:** the audit scripts ran on frozen artifacts and verified saved prediction targets match the patch-centre GEBCO labels. AOI coordinates and four official HDF5 signatures were checked; the new experiment used distinct files/splits. No old test points or model results were removed or reweighted. The new training finished at epoch 90 by validation early stopping; source and model limitations remain as stated.
