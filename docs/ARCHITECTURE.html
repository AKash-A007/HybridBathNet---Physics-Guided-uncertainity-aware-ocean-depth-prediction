HybridBathNet Architecture & Pipeline Specification

This document provides a comprehensive technical reference for the HybridBathNet satellite-derived bathymetry (SDB) system. All descriptions, mathematical formulations, variable names, tensor shapes, and architectural diagrams are extracted directly from the active codebase (src/preprocess.py, src/features.py, src/hybridbathnet.py, src/cnn_baseline.py, and config.yaml).

1. Data Flow Overview

The HybridBathNet pipeline ingests raw multi-spectral satellite imagery and coarse global bathymetry grids, transforming them into high-resolution, physics-regularized coastal depth predictions with calibrated aleatoric and epistemic uncertainty bounds.

Key Changes from Earlier Design Drafts

* Dual Physics Integration Stages: - Pre-Encoder Feature Stack: Analytical Stumpf ratio ($B02/B03$) and Beer-Lambert $z_{\text{prior}}$ are computed statically per pixel in src/features.py (lines 34–70) and stacked into channels 4 and 5. - Post-Encoder On-the-Fly Attenuation: Multi-band $K_d$ and optical extinction depth $z_{\text{ext}}$ are recomputed dynamically in src/hybridbathnet.py (lines 43–109) and fused via cross-attention tokens. * Cross-Attention Fusion vs. Gated MLP: The original static gating vector ($\sigma(W \cdot [f_{\text{spatial}}, \text{phys}])$) was upgraded to a 4-head cross-attention mechanism with Pre-LayerNorm transformer blocks. * Residual Encoder with Channel Attention: The plain sequential CNN encoder was upgraded to residual skip blocks with Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE) channel recalibration. * Depth-Stratified Spatial Splitting: To eliminate spatial autocorrelation leakage and depth bias, splitting is performed across atomic $(128 \times 128)$ spatial blocks sorted by depth quantiles.

Technical Specifications & Resampling Decisions

* Target CRS: Projected UTM Coordinate Reference System specified in config.yaml (e.g., EPSG:32643 for Kollam / Kerala coast, UTM Zone 43N). * Spatial Resolution: Exact 10.0 meters per pixel (resolution = 10.0). * Spectral Band Resampling: Resampling.bilinear is used for Sentinel-2 spectral reflectance bands ($B02, B03, B04, B08$) to avoid nearest-neighbor blocky artifacts while interpolating sub-pixel shifts. * SCL Layer Resampling: Resampling.nearest is strictly enforced because Scene Classification is categorical/discrete (SCL_CLEAR_CLASSES = {4, 5, 6, 7}). * GEBCO Bathymetry Resampling: Resampling.bilinear is used to generate smooth, continuous bottom topography gradients without artificial step discontinuities. * Output Stack Layout: data/processed/stack.tif (Float32): - Bands 1–4: Sentinel-2 Composite ($B02, B03, B04, B08$) - Band 5: Resampled GEBCO Depth ($z < 0\text{ m}$ elevation) - Band 6: Binary Valid Mask ($1.0 = \text{valid clear water}$)

3. Physics Layer — Before the Encoder (src/features.py)

Before patch extraction, two physics-derived analytical channels are generated and concatenated with the raw spectral bands.

1. Stumpf Log-Ratio Band Transformation

Implemented in stumpf_ratio(blue, green) (src/features.py:23–28): $$\text{Stumpf Ratio} = \frac{\ln\left(n \cdot R_{\text{blue}}\right)}{\ln\left(n \cdot R_{\text{green}}\right)}$$ * $n = 1000.0$ (fixed positive scaling constant per Stumpf et al., 2003). * $R_{\text{blue}} = \text{clip}(B02, 10^{-6}, \infty)$, $R_{\text{green}} = \text{clip}(B03, 10^{-6}, \infty)$. * Rationale: Exploits differential water absorption between blue ($490\text{ nm}$) and green ($560\text{ nm}$) wavelengths, linearized in log-space.

2. Beer-Lambert Radiative Transfer Analytical Depth Prior ($z_{\text{prior}}$)

Implemented in beer_lambert_depth_prior(blue, green) (src/features.py:34–70): $$K_d(490) = \text{clip}\left(0.0166 + 0.156 \cdot \left(\frac{R_{\text{blue}}}{R_{\text{green}}}\right)^{-1.14}, 0.01, 5.0\right) \quad (\text{m}^{-1})$$ $$I_{\text{surface}} = \max\left(\text{95th percentile}\left(R_{\text{green}} \mid R_{\text{green}} > 10^{-6}\right), 10^{-3}\right)$$ $$\text{transmission\_ratio} = \text{clip}\left(\frac{I_{\text{surface}}}{R_{\text{green}}}, 1.0, 10^4\right)$$ $$z_{\text{prior}} = \frac{1}{2 \cdot K_d} \cdot \ln\left(\text{transmission\_ratio}\right) \quad (\text{meters})$$ * $2 \cdot K_d$ accounts for the two-way optical attenuation path (downwelling solar radiance + upwelling bottom reflection).

4. CNN Encoder + Attention Blocks (src/hybridbathnet.py)

The spatial feature extractor is a deep Residual Convolutional Network with Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE) channel attention (PhysicsGuidedEncoder, src/hybridbathnet.py:165–232).

Structural Components

1. Squeeze-and-Excitation Block (SEBlock, src/hybridbathnet.py:116–139): * Global Pooling: $\mathbf{z} = \text{AdaptiveAvgPool2d}(1)(\mathbf{X}) \in \mathbb{R}^{C}$ * Bottleneck MLP: $\mathbf{s} = \sigma\left(\mathbf{W}_2 \cdot \text{ReLU}(\mathbf{W}_1 \cdot \mathbf{z})\right)$ where $\mathbf{W}_1 \in \mathbb{R}^{\frac{C}{8} \times C}, \mathbf{W}_2 \in \mathbb{R}^{C \times \frac{C}{8}}$ * Channel Scaling: $\tilde{\mathbf{X}} = \mathbf{X} \odot \mathbf{s}$ 2. Residual Block (ResidualBlock, src/hybridbathnet.py:145–163): $$\mathbf{y} = \text{ReLU}\left(\mathbf{x} + \text{SEBlock}\left(\text{BN}\left(\text{Conv}_{3\times3}\left(\text{Dropout2d}\left(\text{ReLU}\left(\text{BN}\left(\text{Conv}_{3\times3}(\mathbf{x})\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)$$

5. Physics-Constrained Decoder (src/hybridbathnet.py)

The physics decoder transforms spatial features $\mathbf{f}_{\text{spatial}}$ into physically regularized representations $\mathbf{f}_{\text{physics}}$ using optical extinction bounding and multi-head cross-attention (CrossAttentionPhysicsFusion, src/hybridbathnet.py:238–342).

Step 1: Dynamic Multi-Band QAA Attenuation & Extinction Bound

Implemented in compute_kd_and_extinction (src/hybridbathnet.py:43–109): $$K_{d, bg} = 0.0166 + 0.156 \cdot \left(\frac{R_{\text{blue}}}{R_{\text{green}}}\right)^{-1.14} \quad [\text{clear-water component}]$$ $$K_{d, red} = 0.430 \cdot \left(\frac{R_{\text{red}}}{R_{\text{green}} + 10^{-6}}\right) \quad [\text{turbidity component}]$$ $$K_d = \text{mean}_{H, W}(K_{d, bg} + K_{d, red}) \in [0.01, 6.0] \quad (\text{m}^{-1})$$ $$z_{\text{ext}} = \text{clip}\left(\frac{\ln(I_0 / \epsilon)}{2 \cdot K_d + 10^{-6}}, 1.0, 50.0\right) \quad (\text{meters}, \ I_0/\epsilon = 100.0)$$

Step 2: Physical Bounding

$$\mathbf{f}_{\text{nonneg}} = \text{Softplus}(\mathbf{f}_{\text{spatial}}) \ge 0 \quad (\text{enforces non-negative water depth})$$ $$\mathbf{f}_{\text{bounded}} = \min\left(\mathbf{f}_{\text{nonneg}}, z_{\text{ext}} \cdot \mathbf{1}_{128}\right) \quad (\text{constrains features to optical penetration limit})$$

Step 3: Multi-Head Cross-Attention Token Fusion

1. Physics Token Embedding: $$\mathbf{P}_{\text{flat}} = \text{Linear}_{64 \rightarrow (4 \times 128)}\left(\text{ReLU}\left(\text{Linear}_{2 \rightarrow 64}\left([K_d, z_{\text{ext}}]\right)\right)\right) \implies \mathbf{P}_{\text{tokens}} \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 4 \times 128}$$ 2. Cross-Attention Interaction: $$\mathbf{Q} = \text{LayerNorm}(\mathbf{f}_{\text{bounded}}).unsqueeze(1) \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 1 \times 128}$$ $$\mathbf{K} = \mathbf{V} = \text{LayerNorm}(\mathbf{P}_{\text{tokens}}) \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 4 \times 128}$$ $$\mathbf{A} = \text{MultiheadAttention}_{h=4}(\mathbf{Q}, \mathbf{K}, \mathbf{V}).squeeze(1) \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 128}$$ 3. Pre-LN Transformer Feed-Forward: $$\mathbf{f}_{\text{out}} = \text{LayerNorm}(\mathbf{f}_{\text{bounded}} + \mathbf{A})$$ $$\mathbf{f}_{\text{physics}} = \text{LayerNorm}\left(\mathbf{f}_{\text{out}} + \text{Linear}_{256 \rightarrow 128}(\text{Dropout}(\text{GELU}(\text{Linear}_{128 \rightarrow 256}(\mathbf{f}_{\text{out}}))))\right) \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 128}$$

6. Dual Prediction Heads (src/hybridbathnet.py)

Both prediction heads branch from the unified 128-D physics-regularized feature vector $\mathbf{f}_{\text{physics}}$. 
               ┌── Linear(128->64) ── ReLU ── Drop(0.1) ── Linear(64->32) ── ReLU ── Linear(32->1) ──► y_pred (Depth)
f_physics ────┤
               └── Linear(128->64) ── ReLU ── Drop(0.1) ── Linear(64->32) ── ReLU ── Linear(32->1) ──► s = ln(sigma^2) [clamp -10, 10]
1. Depth Regression Head (DepthHead, src/hybridbathnet.py:348–365): * Produces scalar predicted depth $\hat{y}_{\text{norm}} \in \mathbb{R}^B$. * Denormalized to physical depth via $\hat{y}_{\text{meters}} = \hat{y}_{\text{norm}} \cdot \sigma_y + \mu_y$. 2. Aleatoric Uncertainty Head (AleatoricUncertaintyHead, src/hybridbathnet.py:367–386): * Produces scalar log-variance $s_i = \ln(\sigma_{a, i}^2) \in [-10.0, 10.0]$. * Quantifies sensor noise, sediment turbidity, and surface glint uncertainty.

7. Composite Loss (src/hybridbathnet.py)

Implemented in CompositePhysicsNLLLoss (src/hybridbathnet.py:471–593).

Per-Sample Depth Weighting ($w_i$)

To counter severe class imbalance where shallow pixels vastly outnumber deep samples: $$w_i = \text{clip}\left(\frac{|y_{i, \text{meters}}|}{10.0}, 0.5, 3.0\right), \quad \tilde{w}_i = \frac{w_i}{\frac{1}{B} \sum_{j=1}^B w_j}$$

Loss Formulations

1. Depth-Weighted Direct Regression Loss ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{data}}$): $$\mathcal{L}_{\text{data}} = \frac{1}{B} \sum_{i=1}^B \tilde{w}_i \cdot \left(\hat{y}_{i, \text{norm}} - y_{i, \text{norm}}\right)^2$$ 2. Depth-Weighted Heteroscedastic Gaussian NLL Loss ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{NLL}}$): $$\mathcal{L}_{\text{NLL}} = \frac{1}{B} \sum_{i=1}^B \tilde{w}_i \cdot \left[ \frac{1}{2} \exp(-s_i) \left(\hat{y}_{i, \text{norm}} - y_{i, \text{norm}}\right)^2 + \frac{1}{2} s_i \right]$$ 3. Physics Non-Negativity Penalty ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{nonneg}}$): $$\mathcal{L}_{\text{nonneg}} = \frac{1}{B} \sum_{i=1}^B \text{ReLU}\left(-\hat{y}_{i, \text{meters}}\right)$$ 4. Beer-Lambert Extinction Limit Bound Penalty ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{ext}}$): $$\mathcal{L}_{\text{ext}} = \frac{1}{B} \sum_{i=1}^B \text{ReLU}\left(\hat{y}_{i, \text{meters}} - z_{\text{ext}, i}\right)$$

Total Loss Equation & Weights

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{total}} = w_{\text{mse}} \cdot \mathcal{L}_{\text{data}} + w_{\text{nll}}(t) \cdot \mathcal{L}_{\text{NLL}} + w_{\text{phys}} \cdot \left(\frac{\mathcal{L}_{\text{nonneg}}}{\sigma_y + 10^{-6}}\right) + w_{\text{ext}} \cdot \left(\frac{\mathcal{L}_{\text{ext}}}{\sigma_y + 10^{-6}}\right)$$ Weights configured in config.yaml: * w_mse ($w_1$) = 1.0 w_nll ($w_2$) = 1.0 (Curriculum Annealed linearly from $0.0 \rightarrow 1.0$ over epochs $1 \dots 20$*) * w_phys ($w_3$) = 0.5 * w_ext ($w_3$) = 0.5
